Rethinking Physical Support in Bed

The geometry of conventional angles usually forces a trade-off between stability and relaxation. By resting horizontally across a partner's torso, gravity does the supportive work instead of tired limbs. This redistribution of weight allows for a completely relaxed posture, letting the upper body drape naturally while the face remains positioned right where it needs to be.

The experience stops feeling like a calisthenic workout and returns to being a shared sensory exchange.

Balancing Comfort and Engagement

Maintaining physical ease without sacrificing closeness requires understanding how body alignment affects leverage and reach.
